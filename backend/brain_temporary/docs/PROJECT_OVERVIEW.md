# GLOBEX AI — Project Overview

## 1. Vision & Tagline
**"Discover the right trade. Verify the risk. Settle with confidence."**

GLOBEX AI is an institutional-grade, AI-powered cross-border B2B trade intelligence and trust layer. It bridges the critical information and trust gap in international commerce by unifying market discovery, counterparty risk assessment, regulatory compliance, multi-document OCR cross-verification, programmable crypto escrow, and IoT shipment tracking into a single cohesive platform.

## 2. Core Trade Lifecycle
International trade on GLOBEX follows a rigorous, tamper-evident 9-stage pipeline:
```
DISCOVER → MATCH → ASSESS → COMPLY → VERIFY → ESCROW → SHIP → SETTLE
```

1. **DISCOVER**: Identify high-yield international export/import opportunities and demand trends across global markets.
2. **MATCH**: AI semantic matching maps natural-language buyer requirements to verified exporters with high compatibility.
3. **ASSESS**: Dynamic multi-factor risk scoring evaluating financial stability, transaction consistency, and regulatory risk.
4. **COMPLY**: Automated HS code classification, tariff computation, non-tariff barrier (NTM) checks, and mandatory certificate lists.
5. **VERIFY**: OCR extraction and cross-document discrepancy detection across Commercial Invoices, Bills of Lading, and Packing Lists, anchored with SHA-256 hashes on-chain.
6. **ESCROW**: Programmable, multi-signature EVM smart contract locks buyer USDC stablecoin collateral under strict release conditions.
7. **SHIP**: Real-time multi-modal logistics tracking and customs milestone updates.
8. **SETTLE**: Automatic condition satisfaction triggers smart contract execution to release funds directly to the exporter.
*(If a dispute arises, the Human-in-the-loop Arbitrator engine arbitrates with AI-synthesized evidence.)*

## 3. Core Trust Philosophy
- **Verified Facts on Blockchain**: Immutable proof of document hashes, milestone events, and escrow contract state.
- **AI-Powered Interpretation**: Continuous translation of verifiable evidence into explainable Trust (0–100) and Risk scores.
- **Human Authority**: AI assists and surfaces anomalies, but human arbitrators always make final binding dispute rulings.
