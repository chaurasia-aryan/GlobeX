# Trade Data Limitations, Methodological Boundaries & Governance

To maintain empirical rigor and institutional integrity, this document delineates the known boundaries, aggregation assumptions, and methodological limitations of all datasets used in the GLOBEX platform.

---

## 1. Aggregation of Public Trade Data
- **Macro vs Transaction Grain**: UN Comtrade data represents customs-level monthly and annual aggregations at the 6-digit Harmonized System (HS6) level between bilateral trading nations. It does not represent individual commercial invoices, container-level bills of lading, or private business transactions.
- **Valuation Differences**: Export values are typically reported on a Free on Board (FOB) basis, whereas import values are reported on a Cost, Insurance, and Freight (CIF) basis. This standard trade asymmetry creates natural 5–15% discrepancies between reported mirror trade flows that should not be automatically classified as illegal trade fraud.

---

## 2. Nature of Anomaly Labels (Weak & Synthetic vs Ground Truth Fraud)
- **No Direct Fraud Ground Truth**: Public customs datasets do not contain verified judicial or criminal fraud convictions.
- **Weak Supervised Rule Labels**: The labels generated by `scripts/build_anomaly_labels.py` (e.g., `mirror_discrepancy`, `unit_value_outlier`, `sudden_trade_spike`, `quantity_value_mismatch`) represent statistical anomalies and regulatory discrepancy signals, not confirmed legal guilt.
- **Controlled Synthetic Perturbations**: Synthetically generated sequence anomalies are isolated in derived datasets (`features/anomaly_sequences_*.parquet`) and used strictly to benchmark unsupervised and sequence neural models against known distortion patterns.

---

## 3. Document OCR & Information Extraction Datasets
- **Public Domain Proxies**: Standard computer vision and layout understanding datasets (FUNSD, SROIE, CORD, XFUND) consist of marketing receipts, public forms, and general financial documents.
- **Trade-Specific Adaptation**: While these benchmarks establish state-of-the-art token classification and key-value extraction baselines, real maritime trade documents (Bill of Lading, Phytosanitary Certificate, Form A Certificate of Origin) feature specialized stamps, multi-language watermarks, and container grid structures requiring domain-specific fine-tuning.

---

## 4. Entity Registry & Sanctions Coverage
- **Jurisdictional Variability**: GLEIF LEI adoption is comprehensive across financial institutions and European/US corporate entities, but voluntary or emerging in certain South Asian and Latin American small-to-medium enterprise (SME) exporters.
- **Probabilistic Entity Matching**: Sanctions and corporate registry resolution utilizes fuzzy multi-attribute algorithms (token set distance, phonetic matching, address normalization). Matches with scores below 100.0 require human compliance officer review before initiating trade embargo actions.

---

## 5. Tariff Schedules & HS Version Drift
- **Nomenclature Evolution**: The World Customs Organization updates the Harmonized System nomenclature every 5 years (HS 2012, HS 2017, HS 2022). Historical sequence modeling across multiple cycles requires explicit correlation tables to prevent artificial commodity classification shifts.
- **Preferential Rates**: Bilateral preferential tariff schedules (such as India-UAE CEPA or US-MCA) depend on verified Rules of Origin (ROO) compliance and value-addition thresholds that must be validated at time of shipment.
